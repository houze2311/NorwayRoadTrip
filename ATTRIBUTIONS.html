The photographers.

Official tourism photography remains linked because reusable permissions were not established for this private presentation. Visit Norway says to contact its team before using site photographs; Fjord Norway’s image bank generally serves the tourism trade, organisers and press. Norwegian Scenic Routes permits specified uses subject to credits, but its image host could not be downloaded in this environment. The local photographs below are individually licensed Commons alternatives, selected manually, never searched at runtime.
