Road tolls. Toll stations operate automatically. A vehicle tag or a camera reading the registration plate identifies the vehicle; payment follows through an agreement or invoice. There is usually no toll booth to stop at.

Ferry payments. Participating ferries use tags or registration-plate recognition. AutoPASS ferry agreements and FerryPay are different products; FerryPay does not give a fare discount. Check the operator because some ferries use separate tickets or reservations.

Rental cars. Ask how the rental company bills tolls and ferries, what administration fees apply, and whether its existing agreement covers your crossings. Check before registering the rental plate yourself, and limit any registration to your rental dates.

Schedules and closures. Start with Entur, then confirm vehicle-ferry sailings and reservations with the operator. Check Vegvesen Trafikk for closures and seasonal mountain-road access. Forecasts, road status and schedules are live information; these links need internet.
